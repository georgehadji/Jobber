## Section 1 — One customer

*Installs belief 6a. Kills "am I the product?"*

> In this category the job seeker is usually not the customer. Boards are paid by employers.
> Agencies are paid by employers. Free tools are paid by whoever is buying the data.
>
> Workler is paid by you, and by nobody else. There is no employer paying for placement, no
> recruiter buying your details, no advertiser.
>
> That is not a promise about our intentions. It is a structural fact with a consequence you can
> check: there are no employer-side features, because there is no employer-side customer to build
> them for. The repository is open, and an employer dashboard is not a thing that can be hidden in
> it.

## Section 2 — Three things it will not do

*Installs belief 1 and the fabrication rule.*

> **It will not send anything.** Workler drafts your CV, your covering letter, your form answers
> and your follow-ups. You send them. There is a test in the repository that fails if this ever
> stops being true.
>
> **It will not invent anything.** It reorders, reframes and emphasises what is already in your
> CV. It does not add an achievement you did not describe or a tool you did not use.
>
> **It will not flatter you.** Below 4.0 out of 5 it recommends you don't apply. A tool that never
> returns a bad score is not measuring anything.

Claim-with-receipt on all three, each linking to the file that enforces it.

## Section 3 — What we don't know yet

*This section is an asset. Every competitor's compliance badge is a claim; this is a checkable
statement of uncertainty, and segment C reads the difference.*

> The EU AI Act treats employment-related AI as high-risk. The rules were written with employers
> and recruiters in view — the systems that filter candidates — and whether a tool used *by* the
> candidate falls inside them is not settled.
>
> We are not going to put a compliance badge on that. When our lawyers answer it, the answer will
> be on this page with a date on it, and if the answer is that Workler is in scope, we will say so
> and say what changed.
