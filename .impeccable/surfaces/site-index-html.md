# Surface brief: Jobber landing (site/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: any job seeker, evening, many tabs open, wary of AI tools that harvest data. Action: upload a CV and start a first check. Constraints: hosted tool, CV processed and discarded, no testimonials or numbers invented, never claim "local" for the hosted tool, plain HTML/CSS/JS, WCAG 2.2 AA.
Unresolved: daily limit number, provider name, domain, final privacy text (legal review).

## Direction contract

THESIS: One viewport that explains and starts: headline plus the real upload. The category's expected arrangement played at full craft, refusing AI hype imagery, gradients and decorative glow.

OWN-WORLD: Warm stone ground, warm near-black ink, olive display headline and wordmark; orange is the single accent: the primary button, and a darkened orange for links, selected states and the example score. Dark mode keeps the same warm hue. Instrument Serif display, Inter body. Flat surfaces, one hairline border as the only elevation, no shadows. Light and dark follow the system setting. Dropzone, buttons and inputs are rebuilt in this vocabulary.

STORY: Within seconds the visitor understands Jobber scores a job against their own CV and tells them when not to apply. They believe it because what happens to their CV and the tool's limits are stated plainly beside the upload. They drop a CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header: wordmark left, a few text links, "Upload your CV" right. At 1440 two columns: left seven columns carry the headline (about 72px serif), two plain sentences and the privacy line; right five columns the upload panel with the real drop zone and one orange primary button. Below 900px they stack, upload directly under the subcopy.
