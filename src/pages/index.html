Seven blocks, one score, and a separate check on whether the job is real

Workler reads a job posting and your CV and writes a report: role summary, CV match, level strategy, pay research, what to personalise, interview preparation, and work authorisation. Posting legitimacy is scored on its own, because a job you fit perfectly is still worth nothing if nobody is hiring for it.

The result is a number from 1 to 5. Below 4.0, Workler recommends you don’t apply.
