Ninety days, one payment, and then it stops

The hosted version is sold as a ninety-day pass. You pay once. It does not renew, there is no card kept on file, and there is nothing to remember to cancel.

This is deliberate, and it costs us money. The median job search runs about eleven weeks, so a monthly subscription in this category makes most of its margin on the month after you were hired — billing a person for a search that already ended. A tool whose whole argument is that it tells you when not to apply cannot also be quietly charging you for a job you already have.

If your search runs longer than ninety days, you buy another pass. That is a decision you make, not one that happens to you.

The two tiers

The command-line version is not a crippled demo of the hosted product. It is the whole judgment, running on your machine, and it stays that way. The paid tier is not a better verdict. It is the same verdict without the setup.

What you are not paying for

No employer is paying us to show you their roles. No recruiter is buying your details. There are no ads.

You are the only customer, which is why there is a price.

What happens when you pay

One payment of €79 buys ninety days of the hosted version. VAT is included in that number, so the figure on this page is the figure you are charged.

Nothing renews. No card is stored. At day ninety the pass ends on its own and you decide, from a standing start, whether to buy another one.

Payment is handled off this site by a merchant of record, which is also why this site has no payment form, no card field and no analytics attached to one.
